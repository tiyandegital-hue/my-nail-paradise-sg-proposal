About

A small paradise,
ten minutes from home.

My Nail Paradise is a neighbourhood one-stop beauty salon in Hougang — built on a simple idea: everything you need for hands, feet, lashes and skin, in one calm place, without the trip downtown.

Why "Paradise"

One stop.
Zero rushing.

Manicure, pedicure, spa, nail art, lash extensions, waxing, facial and hair removal — the menu grew the way regulars asked for it: "can you also do…?" So now, one appointment can cover the lot.

  • ✓Neighbourhood-first — in the heart of Hougang, walkable for locals.
  • ✓Every pace — express 30-minute touch-ups to full spa afternoons.
  • ✓Straightforward — clear menu, confirmed prices, no hard sell.
