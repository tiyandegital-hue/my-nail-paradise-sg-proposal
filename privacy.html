Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This is a plain-language summary of how My Nail Paradise handles your information.

What we collect

  • Booking requests: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, preferred service, date and time, and any notes you add — only what is needed to arrange your appointment.
  • Direct contact: if you call or WhatsApp us, we see the number you contact us from.

Why we collect it

To confirm, reschedule or follow up on your appointment — nothing else. We do not sell your details, and we do not share them with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Booking details are kept for as long as needed to manage your visits and any reasonable follow-up, then removed on request.

Your rights

You may ask at any time what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — message or call 6386 4385.

Third parties

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. WhatsApp and phone links open the respective apps, which have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This concept site sets no tracking cookies of its own.
